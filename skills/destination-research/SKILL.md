# Dynamic Destination Research Guide

## Pre-trip Research
- **Visa Requirements**: Always perform a live search for the latest visa requirements for the user's nationality entering the destination country.
- **Best Travel Seasons**: Search for the best time to visit based on weather and tourist seasons.
- **Airport Connectivity**: Identify the main international airport(s) and typical routing from the user's origin.

## Cost & Attraction Discovery
- **Top Attractions**: Search for the top-rated attractions at the destination. Find current entrance fees.
- **Local Cuisine**: Search for must-try local dishes and highly-rated restaurants.
- **Local Transport**: Determine the best ways to get around (e.g., Grab, public transit, taxis) and general costs.

## Practical Tips
- **Currency**: Identify the local currency and check current conversion rates dynamically.
- **Safety**: Search for any current travel advisories or safety tips for the destination.
