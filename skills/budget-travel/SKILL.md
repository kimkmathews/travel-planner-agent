# Budget Travel Planning — Reference Guide

## Currency conversions (verify current rates via search before using)
- Always search for exchange rates on the day of planning, not from memory.

## Reliable price sources by category
| Category | Preferred sources |
|---|---|
| Flights | Google Flights, Skyscanner, MakeMyTrip |
| Hotels | Booking.com, Agoda, Hotels.com |
| Activities | Official attraction websites, GetYourGuide, Klook |
| Restaurants | TripAdvisor, Google Maps reviews |

## Budget allocation guidelines
- Flights: 30-40% of total budget
- Accommodation: 18-22% of total budget  
- Meals: 14-18% of total budget
- Activities: 18-25% of total budget
- Transport + misc: 6-10% of total budget

## Rules for cost reporting
1. Every price must have a source URL.
2. State the original currency first, then INR equivalent.
3. If a price cannot be found, write "PRICE NOT FOUND — estimate required" rather than inventing a number.
4. Children under 3 are typically free for activities and often discounted on domestic flights.
