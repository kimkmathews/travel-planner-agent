# Family Travel — Guidelines for Trips with Children

## Age-appropriate activity assessment
- Under 3: Avoid long boat rides (>45 min), high-altitude cable cars, crowded markets at night.
- 3-7: Fine for most beach activities, safari parks, aquariums, theme parks. Limit walking to <3km/day.
- 8-12: Can handle most activities. Engage them in cultural experiences with interactive elements.

## Daily planning rules for families
- Start no earlier than 8:00 AM to allow children time to eat breakfast calmly.
- Build in a 1-1.5 hour rest period after lunch.
- Keep the first day light — children are fatigued after long-haul flights.
- Evening activities should end by 9:00 PM for families with young children.

## Packing essentials for family travel
- High-SPF sunscreen
- Insect repellent
- First-aid kit with antihistamine, antidiarrheal, and paracetamol
